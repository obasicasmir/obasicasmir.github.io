01 · Research

Finding structure
in crowded skies.

I combine near-infrared surveys, astrometry, spectroscopy, and statistical methods to identify stellar systems and recover the physical record encoded in their motions and chemistry.

R / 01

Galactic archaeology

Hidden stellar clusters

Confirming and characterising faint clusters behind the dust and crowding of the inner Milky Way using VVV/VVVX and Gaia.

R / 02

Stellar spectroscopy

Chemistry & motion

Measuring radial velocities, metallicities, and chemical abundances to separate cluster members from the Galactic field.

R / 03

Methods

Detection at survey scale

Building reproducible pipelines and non-parametric methods for finding stellar overdensities across deep astronomical surveys.

04 · About

An observer,
a builder,
a teacher.

I study the fossil record of galaxy formation preserved in star clusters—systems whose ages, chemistry, and orbits connect the present-day sky to the assembly history of galaxies.

I earned my PhD in Astrophysics at Universidad Andrés Bello, with a thesis on low-luminosity Galactic clusters and the globular-cluster system of Circinus. My current research extends this work through spectroscopy, chemo-dynamics, and scalable analysis methods.
